Jozi Nites — The Safest Adult Playground in Mzansi

Community spaces, education, events and support for adults exploring kink, connection and pleasure with confidence.

Who We Are

Jozi Nites is a grassroots non-profit dedicated to creating safe, consensual, and education-led spaces for adults to connect, learn, and celebrate pleasure without shame or stigma.

Our Programs

Wellness Workshops

Weekly meditation, consent practices and wellbeing sessions aimed at building supportive communities.

Game Nite & Socials

Regular social events to meet people in a relaxed, non-judgmental environment.

Academy

Educational workshops and online resources covering safety, consent and play techniques for beginners and experienced adults.

Spaza & Peer Support

Community resource hub and peer listening for adults seeking connection and advice.

Your Spicy Glossary

Mzansi Adult Glossary — No Vanilla Edition

AFTERCARE — Cuddles, water, chocolate, and sweet talk after a scene. Non-negotiable.
BRAT — Sub who misbehaves on purpose just to get "punished". Cute but chaotic.
DROP — The emotional/physical crash after intense play. Can be sub-drop or dom-drop.
EDGE PLAY — Stuff close to the line (breath play, knives, fire). High risk, higher trust.
SAFE WORD — Instant full-stop word. Ours defaults: RED = stop, YELLOW = slow down, GREEN = loving it.
SSC — Safe, Sane, Consensual. The beginner-friendly motto.
