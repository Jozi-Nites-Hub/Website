Who We Are

Jozi Nites is a grassroots non-profit dedicated to creating safe, consensual, and education-led spaces for adults exploring kink, intimacy and alternative communities across Johannesburg (Mzansi). We run events, peer-led workshops and offer resources to build connection and safety.
