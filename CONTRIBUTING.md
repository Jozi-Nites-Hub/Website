# Contributing to Jozi Nites Website

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Jozi Nites**!

This project is the official website for Jozi Nites — a South African Non-Profit Company focused on education, destigmatisation, and community around adult content, creators, and related wellness topics.

We welcome contributions of all kinds: bug fixes, design improvements, new sections, accessibility upgrades, and documentation.

---

## Code of Conduct

By participating in this project, you agree to:

- Be respectful and professional
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or toxic behaviour
- Keep discussions focused and constructive
- Remember that this project deals with sensitive adult content and community topics

Violations may result in removal of contributions or being blocked from the repository.
